# Download invoices and receipts

Workspace owners and billing administrators can open **Settings → Billing → Invoices** to download PDF invoices and payment receipts. Members without a billing role cannot view this page.

## Invoice availability

A paid invoice appears after the payment is captured. Monthly invoices are usually available within ten minutes; annual bank-transfer invoices can remain pending until the transfer clears. KnowledgeDesk does not send invoice attachments by email.

## Billing details

Company name, address, and tax identifiers must be updated before the next invoice is issued. Previously finalized invoices are not automatically regenerated. Billing support can review a correction request but cannot alter transaction amounts.
