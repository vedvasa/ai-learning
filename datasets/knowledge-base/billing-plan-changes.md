# Upgrade or downgrade a workspace plan

Workspace owners can change plans from **Settings → Billing → Plan**. An upgrade takes effect immediately, while a downgrade is scheduled for the end of the current billing period.

## Upgrade charges

The upgrade charge is prorated for the remaining time in the billing period. The checkout screen shows the amount before confirmation. Annual workspaces remain on an annual billing cycle after upgrading.

## Downgrade preparation

Features above the new plan’s limits become unavailable when the downgrade takes effect. Data is not immediately deleted, but automations over the new limit are paused. Export important reports and reduce member or automation counts before the renewal date.
