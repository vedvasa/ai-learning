# Hardware shipping regions

KnowledgeDesk security keys can be shipped to addresses in the contiguous United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and most countries in the European Union. Availability is confirmed during checkout.

## Unsupported destinations

Orders cannot be sent to freight forwarders, sanctioned destinations, or regions where the carrier cannot provide tracked delivery. PO boxes are accepted only when the selected carrier supports them.

## Duties and taxes

The checkout total includes duties when KnowledgeDesk is the importer of record. Otherwise, the recipient may owe import charges to the local carrier. Digital subscriptions do not require shipping and are unaffected by hardware-region restrictions.
