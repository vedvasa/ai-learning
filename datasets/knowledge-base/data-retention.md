# Data retention and workspace deletion

Active workspace content remains available while the subscription is in good standing. Deleted tickets remain recoverable for 30 days before permanent deletion. Audit events are retained for the period provided by the workspace plan.

## Workspace deletion

Only a workspace owner can request deletion from **Settings → Workspace → Delete**. The owner must reauthenticate and confirm the workspace identifier. The workspace enters a seven-day cancellation window before deletion begins.

## Backups and exports

Encrypted backups may retain deleted records for up to 35 additional days before rotation. Backups are used for disaster recovery and are not restored to answer individual record-recovery requests. Owners should export required records before confirming deletion.
