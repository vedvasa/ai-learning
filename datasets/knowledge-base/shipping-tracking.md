# Track a hardware order

The ordering administrator receives a tracking email after the carrier scans the package. Tracking also appears under **Settings → Orders**. A label-created status means the package has not yet received its first carrier scan.

## Delayed scans

Allow one business day after shipment for tracking activity. International packages may pause while customs processes the shipment. KnowledgeDesk cannot change the delivery address after the carrier accepts the package.

## Missing delivery

If tracking shows delivered but the package is missing, check the reception desk and neighboring delivery locations first. Report the issue within seven days so support can open a carrier investigation. Replacement approval depends on the investigation result.
