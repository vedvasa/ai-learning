# Cancel a subscription

A workspace owner can cancel from **Settings → Billing → Plan**. Cancellation prevents the next renewal and does not immediately delete workspace data.

## Access after cancellation

Paid features remain available through the end of the current billing period. The workspace then moves to the eligible free plan or becomes read-only when its usage exceeds free limits. Cancellation does not itself create a refund.

## Before the billing period ends

Export required reports, review retention requirements, and reduce usage that exceeds the destination plan. The owner can reverse a scheduled cancellation before the renewal date. A deleted workspace cannot be restored through subscription reactivation.
