# Starter plan limits

The Starter plan supports up to five workspace members, three active automations, and 5 GB of attachment storage. It includes 30 days of audit history and community support.

## Limit behavior

New members or automations cannot be added after a limit is reached. Existing automations continue to run unless a downgrade placed the workspace above the limit. Storage uploads are blocked when the allowance is full, but existing attachments remain readable.

## Reducing usage

Remove inactive members, archive unused automations, or delete unneeded attachments. Storage totals can take up to 15 minutes to refresh after deletion. Upgrading the plan raises limits immediately after payment succeeds.
