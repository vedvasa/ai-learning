# Refund eligibility

First-time subscription purchases may be eligible for a refund when the workspace owner contacts billing support within 14 calendar days of the charge. Renewal charges and usage-based charges are normally non-refundable.

## Request information

Include the workspace identifier, invoice number, charge date, and a short reason. Never send card numbers or bank credentials. Billing support verifies account ownership before reviewing the request.

## Exceptions

Duplicate charges and confirmed processing errors are investigated regardless of the 14-day window. Refund approval is not automatic. Taxes and currency conversion differences may be handled separately by the payment processor or local tax authority.
