# Service status and incident updates

The public status page reports confirmed incidents affecting sign-in, ticket processing, notifications, file uploads, and APIs. Customers can subscribe to email or webhook updates for individual components.

## Incident lifecycle

An incident moves through investigating, identified, monitoring, and resolved states. Update intervals depend on severity, but critical incidents receive an update at least every 30 minutes even when there is no material change.

## Support tickets during incidents

Check the status page before opening a duplicate ticket. A ticket is still appropriate when the observed behavior differs from the posted incident or when support needs account-specific details. Post-incident reviews are published for major incidents after internal review.
