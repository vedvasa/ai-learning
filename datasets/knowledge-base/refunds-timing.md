# Refund processing times

After KnowledgeDesk approves a refund, the payment processor submits it to the original payment method. The billing page changes the transaction status to **Refunded** and shows the approved amount.

## Card and wallet payments

Card and digital-wallet refunds commonly appear within five to ten business days. The exact posting date is controlled by the customer’s financial institution. Support cannot accelerate a refund after the processor accepts it.

## Bank transfers

Approved bank-transfer refunds can require up to 15 business days and may require verified recipient details. If the expected window has passed, contact billing support with the invoice number and refund confirmation; do not send bank credentials in a support ticket.
