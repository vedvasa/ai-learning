# Manage your account profile

KnowledgeDesk account owners can change the display name, contact email, locale, and time zone from **Settings → Profile**. Profile changes apply to the individual user and do not rename the workspace.

## Changing an email address

Enter the new address and select **Send verification**. The new address becomes active only after its verification link is opened. The link expires after 30 minutes. If the old address is unavailable, contact a workspace administrator; support cannot bypass ownership verification.

## Workspace names

Workspace owners rename a workspace from **Settings → Workspace → General**. Existing bookmarks continue to work because the workspace identifier does not change. Audit history records the owner who made the change.
